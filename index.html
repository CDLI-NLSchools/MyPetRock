My Pet Rock

Grade 7 STEAM, Explore the Land, Lesson 3 Extension

Every pet rock needs a portrait. Choose a rock, decorate it, place it in a scene, and tell us all about it. You can do the whole thing on this page, or decorate a real rock at home and use a photo of it here.
